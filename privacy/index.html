SocialPublisher Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

Information processed

SocialPublisher processes account authorization information, selected media, captions, publishing preferences and publishing status required to provide the service.

How information is used

Information is used only to connect authorized social accounts, upload user-selected media, publish content and show publishing status to the user.

Credentials

Platform access credentials are transmitted securely to the SocialPublisher server and stored in encrypted form. Credentials are not included in public logs or published media URLs.

Sharing

Required media and publishing instructions are sent to the social platforms selected by the user. Information is not sold.

Retention

Media and operational records are retained only as needed to provide publishing, status, security and recovery functions.

User choices

Users may disconnect a platform account and revoke authorization through SocialPublisher or the relevant social platform.

Contact

Privacy questions may be sent to the contact email registered for the SocialPublisher developer application.
