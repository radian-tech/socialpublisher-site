SocialPublisher Terms of Service

Service

Users must own or have permission to publish all submitted media. Users are responsible for captions, privacy choices, disclosures, permissions and compliance with each social platform's rules.

Authorization

SocialPublisher only publishes after the user selects a destination and explicitly confirms the publishing action. Users may disconnect a connected platform account.

Prohibited use

Users must not publish unlawful, misleading, infringing, abusive or unauthorized content.

Availability

Publishing may be delayed or rejected by a social platform, network failure, account restriction or platform API limitation.
